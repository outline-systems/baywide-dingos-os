FAQ

Practical answers before you test it.

No inflated claims. Just what the system is designed to do and how it fits into a real service business.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. AI Front Desk is designed to add coverage around your existing team. Your people stay in control; the AI catches calls they cannot answer.

What happens to my existing business number?

You can keep it. Calls can be forwarded after hours, on unanswered calls, during overflow, or you can use a separate AI number if that better suits your setup.

Can I choose which calls the AI answers?

Yes. Call handling is configured around your rules and chosen coverage windows rather than assuming the AI should answer everything.

What happens with urgent calls?

The call flow can identify configured urgency signals and escalate or transfer urgent jobs to an on-call person when that process has been set up.

Does it only work after hours?

No. After-hours coverage is one use case. It can also handle overflow or unanswered calls during the day when the office or field team is busy.

Can I hear it before I pay for anything?

Yes. The custom demo is free and carries no obligation. We build a sample call flow around your actual business so you can call it and judge the experience yourself.
