FAQ

Practical answers before you test it.

No inflated claims. Just how the system fits into a real service business.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No. It adds coverage around your existing team. Your people stay in control.

What happens to my existing business number?

You can keep it. Calls can forward after hours, on unanswered calls, during overflow, or you can use a separate AI number.

Can I choose which calls the AI answers?

Yes. Handling is configured around your rules and chosen coverage windows.

What happens with urgent calls?

The flow can identify configured urgency signals and escalate or transfer urgent jobs to an on-call person when set up.

Does it only work after hours?

No. It can also handle overflow or unanswered calls during the day.

Can I hear it before I pay for anything?

Yes. The custom demo is free and carries no obligation. We build it around your actual business so you can call it yourself.
