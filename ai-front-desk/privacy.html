Privacy Policy

How information is handled through the AI Front Desk website and custom demo process.

Last updated: September 6, 2026.

AI Front Desk is a customer-facing service operated by Outline Projects. This policy applies to information collected through the AI Front Desk marketing website, including custom demo requests. Service-specific privacy terms may also apply if you later become a customer.

Information we collect

When you request a custom demo, we may collect your first name, business name, website, email address, phone number, industry, city and current business hours. Hosting or analytics providers may also process standard technical information such as page requests, browser/device information, referring page and approximate location derived from IP address where available.

How we use information

  • To review your business and build the custom call-flow demo you requested.
  • To contact you about the demo and possible implementation.
  • To improve the website and understand usage where analytics are enabled.
  • To prevent spam, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

Demo preparation

If you provide a business website, we may review publicly available information on that site to understand your services, service area, hours and common customer questions for the purpose of preparing the requested demo.

Service providers

We may use third-party providers for hosting, form delivery, email, file storage and analytics. These providers may process information in countries other than your own. We limit use to information reasonably necessary for the relevant function.

Call recording and transcription

Recording or transcription is optional and is not assumed for every AI Front Desk deployment. If enabled for a customer, disclosure and consent requirements must be configured for the laws applicable to the customer, caller and jurisdiction.

Cookies and analytics

The initial site can operate without advertising cookies. Analytics or advertising tags may be added later. Where applicable law requires consent, an appropriate consent mechanism will be implemented.

Retention

Demo-request information may be retained as reasonably needed to prepare the demo, follow up, maintain business records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Information no longer needed may be deleted or de-identified.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing. If you already have an agreement with us, use the contact method provided there. Otherwise, you can submit the website form and state “Privacy request” in the Business name field.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information handled. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website, service delivery model or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page.
